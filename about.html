Dust to Desire: The Beginning

Born in the shadows, Obsession Perfumes was founded by Manny Sinclair in 2024 on the belief that scent is the most intimate obsession. Our founders sought to capture the elusive moments of passion, mystery, and raw desire that traditional fragrances often ignored.

Each bottle is more than a scent; it's a meticulously crafted narrative, a complex story told in top notes of curiosity and base notes of lasting desire.
